# Assorted Bridges

Adds bridge controls. Give one a redstone signal and it shoots out a beam of laser light you can walk on. There is a plain laser bridge, a trick bridge you fall straight through, an acceleration bridge that speeds you along, a death bridge that hurts whatever touches it and a gravity lift that pushes things the way it points. Right click a control with a block and the beam takes on that block's look.

Also included in [Assorted Tech](../tech). Worlds made with Assorted Tech 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
