# Assorted Sensors

Adds twelve sensors that give off a redstone signal when something is in front of them. What each one watches for depends on what it is made of, from any entity at all down to only players, monsters, dropped items or flying mobs. It also adds the GPS, which stores a position, and GPS sensors that watch that position from somewhere else. The upgraded GPS sensor reaches farther, watches a wider area and can look for lists of players, mobs or items and their tags.

Also included in [Assorted Tech](../tech). Worlds made with Assorted Tech 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
