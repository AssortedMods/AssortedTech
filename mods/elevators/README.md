# Assorted Elevators

Adds elevators that carry you and anything standing on them up and down a shaft, stopping at every landing you build into its side. Place up to three by three of them side by side and they move together. Instant elevators take you straight to the next one of the same color when you jump or sneak, and a dye changes which ones link up. There is a camouflaged version of each that can be disguised as any solid block.

Also included in [Assorted Tech](../tech). Worlds made with Assorted Tech 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
