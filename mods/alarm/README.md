# Assorted Alarm

Adds an alarm box that goes on any face of a block and sounds when it gets a redstone signal. Right click it to pick one of fourteen alarm sounds. Wire it to a sensor or a pressure plate and you have a doorbell, or something much louder.

Also included in [Assorted Tech](../tech). Worlds made with Assorted Tech 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
