# Assorted Spikes

Adds spikes that go on a floor, a wall or a ceiling. Give one a redstone signal and it pops out and hurts whatever touches it. There is a spike for every vanilla material and for every metal and gem Assorted Core adds, and the better the material the harder it hits.

Also included in [Assorted Tech](../tech). Worlds made with Assorted Tech 9.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
